04 — How I work

Four habits. Boring on paper, lethal in practice.

🚀 /01

Ship fast, fix forward.

Working software beats elegant slides. I'd rather merge today and refactor on Monday than spend two weeks polishing a branch nobody asked for.

📚 /02

Read the codebase first.

Before I open a PR, I read the surrounding files, follow the conventions, find the seams. New code should look like it was always there.

💬 /03

Talk to the user.

Specs lie, users don't. Five minutes with the person doing the job beats an hour of meetings about what the job ought to be.

⚡ /04

Use AI as a tool, not a crutch.

Claude and Cursor compress my output. They don't think for me. Every line ships through my own brain — that's why it works.
