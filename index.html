How I work

Boring on paper. Lethal in production.

Test what matters

Unit, integration, and contract tests on critical paths, Jest, RTL, Pytest. Regressions caught before merge, not in prod.

Instrument day one

Structured logs, metrics, and dashboards in CloudWatch, Prometheus, and Grafana. Production is observable, not guessed at.

Automate the boring

GitHub Actions covering lint, test, build, deploy. Shorter cycles, fewer manual errors, predictable rollouts.

Document & collaborate

Clear service docs, meaningful reviews, decisions made in the open. Teammates onboard without bottlenecks.
