About

Engineer who ships the whole thing.

I'm Gagan Karthik Mullapudi, a Full-Stack Engineer with three-plus years shipping production software across product startups and enterprise consultancies. I own features end-to-end, from React component libraries through REST API contracts and PostgreSQL schemas to the CI/CD pipelines that deploy them on AWS.

Right now I'm at Ocean Blue Solutions in the United States, building three live products: Blue-IQ, a document-intelligence engine that reads contracts and scores every field it extracts; the Ocean Blue Corp corporate platform; and an internal workforce-management app for staffing teams.

What I care about: accessible, performant interfaces that meet WCAG 2.1; clean API contracts with proper auth and validation; databases that scale predictably through query tuning and strategic indexing; and CI/CD that catches problems before production, backed by Jest, React Testing Library, and Pytest on every critical path.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Kennesaw State University and I'm AWS Certified. Outside the terminal, I'm usually reading about systems design or refining something that already works just to make it a little quieter.
